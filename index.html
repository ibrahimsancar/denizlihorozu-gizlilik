Denizli Horozu · Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The "denizlihorozu paylasim" app is used only by the owner of the Instagram account @denizlihorozuyum to manage that account's own content. It is not offered to other users or accounts.

Data we process

  • Posts and insights (reach, views) of @denizlihorozuyum and the public comments written on these posts (comment text, the commenter's public username, timestamp).
  • The account's access token, stored only in a protected file on a server accessible to the account owner.

Why we use it

  • To schedule posts and measure their performance.
  • To prepare and send replies to comments on our account, and to hide abusive or spam comments. To draft a reply, the comment text may be sent to an AI service; the commenter's profile information is not sent.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell the data, use it for advertising or share it with third parties, except that the AI service mentioned above processes the comment text when drafting a reply. We keep comment records as long as the replying workflow needs them and delete them on request.

Data deletion

To have records about a comment you wrote on our account deleted, email ibrahimcsancar@gmail.com with your Instagram username. We will delete the records within a reasonable time. Deleting your comment on Instagram removes your content there; an email is enough to delete our copy.
